# Paste-Ready Artifact Description

[Semantic Research Navigator](https://Farhan2k20.github.io/semantic-research-navigator/)

Semantic Research Navigator is an interactive research prototype for organizing literature review evidence into claims, methods, limitations, and synthesis themes. It presents a practical workflow for comparing paper-level evidence, ranking relevance, and generating concise summaries while preserving a clear source trail. I built it as a shareable artifact to demonstrate interest in AI-assisted research tools, human-centered knowledge organization, and responsible synthesis workflows.
