Artifact

What the prototype demonstrates

Problem

Literature reviews are difficult because relevant evidence is scattered across PDFs, highlights, and notes. The hardest part is not only summarizing papers, but preserving enough structure to compare them responsibly.

Approach

The navigator represents each paper as an evidence card with fields for research claim, method, limitation, and confidence. A synthesis layer groups related evidence into themes and exposes weak spots before writing begins.

Contribution

The artifact shows how AI support can make research synthesis more traceable: every generated theme should point back to specific evidence rather than becoming a detached summary.

Workflow

From question to synthesis

  1. 01

    Frame the question

    Define the research topic, inclusion criteria, and what counts as useful evidence.

  2. 02

    Extract evidence

    Capture each paper's claim, method, dataset, limitation, and confidence level.

  3. 03

    Compare patterns

    Group related claims, separate strong evidence from weak evidence, and identify gaps.

  4. 04

    Write with traceability

    Generate a synthesis that remains grounded in the selected sources and limitations.
